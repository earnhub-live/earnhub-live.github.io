Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EarnHub ("we", "our" or "the platform") handles information when you visit earnhub-live.github.io, create an account, submit tasks, use wallet features or contact support. Please read it before providing personal information.

This policy describes the types of information used to operate the service. The exact information collected depends on the feature you choose to use and the third-party services enabled on the website.

1. Information We May Collect

  • Account information: name or username, email address, authentication identifiers and profile details you provide.
  • Task information: task selections, submissions, proof files, review status and related communications.
  • Payment information: deposit and withdrawal request amounts, payment method, transaction reference, recipient details you enter and payment screenshots you upload. Do not include PINs, passwords or one-time codes in screenshots or messages.
  • Wallet and transaction records: deposit-related balances, earnings, available balance, package orders, reward records and payment-request history.
  • Support information: messages you send to the support team and replies associated with your account.
  • Technical information: information that may be provided by your browser or service providers, such as IP address, device/browser type, approximate location derived from network information, pages visited, timestamps and diagnostic data.

2. How We Use Information

We use information for legitimate platform operations, including to:

  • create and authenticate user accounts;
  • display account features and maintain profile information;
  • receive and review task submissions and apply reward rules;
  • verify deposits, package orders and withdrawal requests;
  • maintain wallet, transaction and support records;
  • send service-related notices and respond to questions;
  • detect fraud, duplicate accounts, abuse and security incidents;
  • maintain, troubleshoot and improve website functionality; and
  • meet applicable legal, accounting and record-keeping requirements.

3. Supabase and Other Service Providers

EarnHub uses Supabase services for authentication, database and related application functions. Information submitted through the website may be processed or stored by Supabase on our behalf, subject to the provider's terms and privacy practices. We may also rely on hosting, security, storage, analytics, email or other providers where needed to operate the platform.

These providers should receive only information needed for their service. Their own privacy policies and terms may also apply to their processing.

4. Google Analytics and Advertising

EarnHub may use Google Analytics 4 to understand general website usage, such as page visits, traffic sources, device/browser categories and interactions. Analytics technologies may use cookies or similar identifiers. Where available, you can manage browser cookie settings and use Google's controls for analytics and advertising.

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled on a page, Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar technologies to serve, measure and limit advertisements, detect invalid activity and, where permitted by your settings and applicable law, personalize ads. Advertising partners may process information about visits to this and other websites.

For information about Google's partner-site data practices, visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can review Google's Privacy Policy and Ad Settings.

EarnHub does not ask users to click advertisements, view advertisements for compensation or otherwise interact with ads to earn task rewards. Users must not generate or encourage artificial ad impressions or clicks.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies and similar technologies may be used for essential website functionality, authentication, security, analytics and advertising. Some cookies may be set by third-party services embedded in or used by the website. You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings; blocking some cookies may affect site functionality.

Where consent is legally required for non-essential cookies or personalized advertising, the relevant consent choices should be presented before those technologies are used. Browser controls alone may not replace a legally required consent mechanism.

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell users' personal information. We may disclose limited information:

  • to service providers that help operate, secure or maintain EarnHub;
  • to payment or communications providers where needed to process a request;
  • to advertising or analytics partners when those services are enabled;
  • when required by law, court order or a valid government request; or
  • to investigate fraud, protect users, enforce our terms or protect platform rights and security.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information. No website, network transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your password private, use a secure device and contact us if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account and financial records, verify transactions, resolve disputes, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations. Some transaction or fraud-prevention records may need to be retained after an account deletion request where permitted or required by law.

9. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information associated with your account, correct inaccurate details where supported, or request account and data deletion. We may need to verify your identity before responding. Some information may be retained where necessary for security, unresolved transactions, legal compliance or legitimate record keeping.

You can manage browser cookies through your browser settings and review Google's advertising controls using the links above. Disabling cookies may affect some website features.

10. Children's Privacy

EarnHub is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly request personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the matter. Users must also meet any higher minimum age or eligibility requirement that applies to a particular service or law.

11. International Processing

Our service providers may process or store information in countries other than the country where you live. Data protection rules may differ between countries. Where applicable, we take steps required by law when information is transferred to another jurisdiction.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, providers or legal requirements change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Please review it periodically.
