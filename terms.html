5. Wallet Balances and Earnings

EarnHub displays separate wallet categories because they have different purposes:

  • Available Balance: the balance used for eligible withdrawal requests, subject to minimums, verification and account conditions.

Do not treat these figures as interchangeable or add deposit funds to available earnings when calculating a withdrawal. Account records and the applicable transaction status determine the current balance.

Package orders may require administrative review and activation. A package is not active until the account shows its approved active status. Package-related funds are separate from ordinary withdrawable task earnings.

Package rewards depend on the plan conditions and platform processing. They must not be represented as a guaranteed investment return, guaranteed profit or guaranteed income. Review the package details carefully and do not purchase a package using money needed for essential expenses.
