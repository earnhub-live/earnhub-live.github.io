👤 Account & Login

How do I create an EarnHub account?
  1. Open the official EarnHub website.
  2. Select Sign Up and enter your username/full name as requested.
  3. Enter a valid email address and create a password.
  4. Complete registration and follow any verification prompt.
  5. Log in to open your account dashboard.
Use your own accurate details and keep your password private.
How do I log in?
Open EarnHub, select Login, enter the email address and password associated with your account, and submit. Check for extra spaces if login fails.
I forgot my password. What should I do?
Use the Forgot Password link on the login form and follow the password-reset email. Check your spam folder. Never send your password or reset link to another person.
Can I use EarnHub without logging in?
You can browse public information pages. Account functions such as task submissions, wallet requests and support history require an authenticated account.
Can I create multiple accounts?
Do not create duplicate or misleading accounts to obtain additional rewards or bypass limits. Use one legitimate account and contact support if you have lost access.

📋 Tasks & Rewards

How do I complete a task?
  1. Log in and open the Tasks section.
  2. Choose a task that is available to your account.
  3. Read all instructions, eligibility rules and proof requirements before starting.
  4. Open the task link if one is provided and complete the requested activity.
  5. Return to EarnHub, enter genuine completion proof and submit it.
  6. Keep the submission status available for reference while it is reviewed.
What is task proof?
Task proof is the information requested to help verify completion. It may be a text response, reference or screenshot depending on the task. Submit only genuine proof and do not include unrelated private information.
What is the normal task reward?
The current normal task reward is PKR 50 for an approved task. The task must be completed correctly and pass review. The reward is not credited merely for opening a task.
Are sponsored task rewards different?
Yes. A sponsored campaign may display a reward amount different from a normal task. Review the campaign's own instructions, remaining slots, eligibility and proof requirements before participating.
Why is my task submission pending?
Pending means your proof has been submitted and is awaiting review. A pending submission is not yet an approved reward. Check the dashboard for a later status update.
When is a task reward credited?
A reward is credited after the submission is approved under the task rules. Check the wallet and transaction history after approval. If the record does not appear, contact support with the task name and submission date.
Why was my task rejected?
A submission may be rejected if proof is missing, unclear, inaccurate, duplicated or inconsistent with the task instructions. Review the rejection information and contact support if you believe there was an error.
Can I submit the same task repeatedly?
The task system may apply a 24-hour restriction to another submission for the same task. Follow the message shown in your account; do not create another account to bypass a limit.
Does EarnHub guarantee that tasks will always be available?
No. Task supply depends on platform activity and, for sponsored campaigns, sponsor availability and campaign slots. Earnings are not guaranteed.

💳 Deposits

What is the minimum deposit?
The published minimum deposit is PKR 300. Check the live Deposit page before sending money in case the platform has announced a change.
How do I submit a deposit request?
  1. Log in and open the Deposit section.
  2. Enter an amount that meets the displayed minimum.
  3. Select one of the payment methods currently shown in your account.
  4. Pay only to the recipient and account details displayed by EarnHub.
  5. Enter the transaction reference and upload the requested receipt.
  6. Submit the request and retain your payment proof until it is resolved.
Which deposit payment methods are supported?
Payment methods can vary by payment flow and may be updated. Use only the methods and recipient details displayed in the live EarnHub Deposit page. Do not rely on an old screenshot or send funds to details shared by an unofficial account.
Why is my deposit pending?
A pending request is waiting for payment verification. Keep your transaction ID and receipt. Do not submit repeated requests for the same transfer unless support instructs you to.
Why was my deposit rejected?
Possible reasons include an incorrect transaction reference, missing or unclear receipt, amount mismatch, unsupported payment route or inability to verify the transfer. Contact support with the request details if you need clarification.
Why can't I submit another deposit request?
The system may prevent another request while an earlier deposit request is pending. Wait for the existing request to be reviewed or contact support if it remains unresolved.
Are deposits the same as withdrawable earnings?
No. Deposit-related funds are tracked separately and are intended for eligible package activation. They are not automatically added to Available Balance and should not be treated as withdrawable task earnings.
What should my payment screenshot contain?
Upload the requested transaction receipt through the official form. Make sure the relevant amount, date and transaction reference are readable. Do not upload passwords, PINs, one-time codes or unrelated sensitive information.

📦 Packages

What are EarnHub packages?
Packages are optional account products with a displayed price, duration and reward schedule. Read all package details and conditions before requesting activation. A package is not a bank deposit or a guaranteed investment return.
How are packages activated?
A package order is submitted for review and activation according to the current platform process. Check the order status in your account. Do not assume a package is active until the dashboard confirms activation.
How long does a package run?
The currently listed package plans have a 60-day duration. Check the individual plan card and your approved order for its activation and expiry dates.
Where are package rewards shown?
Package rewards are recorded separately from ordinary task rewards. Check the package area and wallet history for the relevant records and status.
Can I withdraw package deposit funds?
Deposit or investment funds are not the same as Available Balance. They are intended for eligible package activation and are not directly withdrawable as task earnings.
Can I request a package refund?
The published refund request window is 24 to 72 hours from purchase. Under the stated package refund condition, a package that has already generated a reward is not eligible. All requests require verification; read the Terms & Conditions for the complete rule.

💸 Withdrawals

What is the minimum withdrawal?
The minimum withdrawal request is PKR 600. Meeting the minimum amount alone does not guarantee eligibility; the account must also pass the platform's balance and deposit checks.
What conditions apply before I can withdraw?
The current process requires an eligible Available Balance and approved deposit eligibility of at least PKR 600, together with the minimum withdrawal amount. The live dashboard and current Terms & Conditions determine whether your account qualifies.
Which withdrawal methods are supported?
The listed withdrawal methods are JazzCash, Easypaisa, UPaisa, NayaPay, SadaPay and bank transfer. Use the exact account details and options shown in your withdrawal form.
How long does a withdrawal take?
The expected processing time is 10–30 minutes after the request is reviewed, subject to verification, workload and payment-service availability. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed completion time.
Is there a withdrawal fee?
EarnHub's published platform withdrawal fee is PKR 0. A payment provider or bank may have its own charges outside EarnHub's control; check with the provider.
Why was my withdrawal rejected or delayed?
Possible reasons include insufficient eligible balance, unmet deposit conditions, incorrect recipient details, verification issues or payment-service delays. Review the request status and contact support with its reference.
Can I withdraw deposit funds directly?
No. Deposit or investment funds are separate from Available Balance and are intended for eligible package activation. Withdrawals are processed from eligible Available Balance under the platform rules.

👛 Wallet & History

What does Total Deposit / Investment mean?
It shows deposit-related funds tracked separately for eligible package activation. It is not the same as task earnings and is not directly withdrawable.
What does Total Earnings mean?
It is the account's recorded earnings total under the platform's rules. It may include earnings that are not currently available for withdrawal, so do not use this figure alone to decide whether a withdrawal will be approved.
What does Available Balance mean?
Available Balance is the wallet amount used for eligible withdrawal requests. Minimum amount, approved-deposit eligibility and account verification rules still apply.
Why are the three wallet figures different?
They represent different categories: deposit-related funds, accumulated earnings and the amount currently eligible for withdrawal. They are intentionally shown separately and should not be added together to estimate a withdrawable amount.
Where can I review transactions?
Open the relevant wallet, deposit, withdrawal or package history area in your account. Keep request references and receipts until each transaction is resolved.

🤝 Referral Program

How does the referral program work?
Your account may provide a referral link or code. Share it with people who want to learn about EarnHub. Referral rewards are conditional and depend on qualifying activity and the current referral rules.
When is a referral commission credited?
The current referral rule provides a PKR 35 commission after the referred user completes a qualifying task that is approved. A registration or link click alone does not qualify.
Can I refer myself or create extra accounts?
No. Self-referrals, duplicate accounts and attempts to manipulate referral tracking are prohibited and may lead to reward rejection or account restrictions.

🔐 Safety & Advertising

How can I protect my account?
  • Use a unique password.
  • Never share your password, PIN or one-time code.
  • Use the official EarnHub website.
  • Check payment details before transferring funds.
  • Log out on shared devices.
  • Contact support through the official Help Center.
Will EarnHub support ask for my password?
No. Never share your password, recovery link, PIN or one-time verification code with anyone, including someone claiming to be support.
Can I submit fake task proof?
No. Submissions must be genuine and follow the task instructions. Fabricated, altered or misleading proof can be rejected and may lead to account restrictions.
Can I earn a reward for clicking or viewing Google ads?
No. EarnHub tasks must not ask users to click advertisements, view ads for compensation, search for ads, or interact with ads to generate a reward. Never click an ad to support a publisher or because another person asks you to. Report any task that appears to request this.
What should I do if someone promises guaranteed profit?
Treat guaranteed-income or urgent-payment claims with caution. Review official package details and refund conditions, do not send money to personal accounts shared in messages, and report suspicious requests.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

The website is not loading. What should I do?
Check your internet connection, refresh the page and try another browser. If the issue continues, note the page URL, approximate time and error message before contacting support.
My task link is not opening.
Check whether your browser blocked a new tab or pop-up. Open only links shown inside the official task instructions and avoid unrelated downloads.
My payment screenshot is not uploading.
Use a JPG, PNG or WEBP image smaller than 5 MB, as required by the current form. Check your connection and try again. Do not send the receipt to an unofficial social account.
I cannot log in.
Check the email address for spelling and extra spaces. Use Forgot Password if needed. If the reset email does not arrive, check spam and contact support without sharing your password.
My support message was sent but I cannot see it.
Refresh the Help Center and check the support history while logged in. If the message is still missing, submit one follow-up with the approximate submission time rather than repeatedly sending the same message.
A wallet or payment record looks incorrect.
Save the request reference, amount, date and relevant receipt. Contact support with these details. Never post full payment account credentials or verification codes.
