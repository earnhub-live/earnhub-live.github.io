👤 Account & Login

How do I create an EarnHub account?
  1. Open the EarnHub home page.
  2. Choose the Sign Up or Register option.
  3. Enter your name, email address and password.
  4. Complete the registration process.
  5. Log in to access your dashboard.

Your account details should be kept private.

How do I log in?
Open EarnHub, select Login, enter your registered email address and password, then submit the form.
Where can I find my dashboard?
After logging in, open your dashboard from the EarnHub website. Your available account information, tasks, earnings and other account features are displayed there.
Can I use EarnHub without logging in?
You may browse the public website, but you need to log in to use account features such as submitting tasks and managing your wallet.

📋 Tasks & Rewards

How do I complete a task?
  1. Log in to your EarnHub account.
  2. Open the available Tasks section.
  3. Choose an active task and read its instructions.
  4. Click Start Task. The task link will open in a new tab.
  5. Complete the task according to its instructions.
  6. Return to EarnHub and click Start Task again.
  7. Enter your completion proof and submit it.
What is task proof?
Task proof is the information requested to show that you completed a task. Enter valid and genuine proof in the submission prompt.
Always follow the specific proof instructions shown for the task.
Why is my task submission pending?
After you submit proof, your submission is saved with a pending status. It must be reviewed by an EarnHub administrator before it can be approved or rejected.
When will I receive my task reward?
A task reward is credited after the task submission is successfully approved. Check your wallet and transaction history for account updates.
Why was my task rejected?
A submission may be rejected if the proof is missing, incorrect, incomplete or does not follow the task instructions. Review the task requirements before submitting again.
Can I submit the same task again?
The current task system may restrict another submission for the same task for 24 hours. If you see a 24-hour message, wait until the restriction has passed.

💳 Deposits

How do I deposit funds?
  1. Log in to your EarnHub account.
  2. Open the Deposit section.
  3. Enter the deposit amount.
  4. Select an available payment method.
  5. Make the payment using the displayed instructions.
  6. Enter your payment Transaction ID.
  7. Upload your payment screenshot.
  8. Submit your deposit request.
Which payment methods are supported for deposits?
The current website code accepts JazzCash and Easypaisa for deposit requests. Use the payment instructions displayed on the Deposit page.
What is the minimum deposit amount?
The current deposit form has a minimum of $3 USD. Check the live Deposit page before making a payment in case the rules have been updated.
Why do I need to upload a payment screenshot?
The screenshot helps the administrator verify your payment. The current form accepts JPG, PNG and WEBP images up to 5 MB.
Why can't I submit another deposit request?
The current system does not allow a new deposit request while you already have a pending deposit request. Wait for the existing request to be processed.
How can I check my deposit status?
Open the Deposit History section in your account to review your submitted deposit requests and their available status information.

💸 Withdrawals

How do I withdraw my earnings?
  1. Log in to your EarnHub account.
  2. Open the Withdraw section.
  3. Enter the withdrawal amount.
  4. Select an available withdrawal method.
  5. Enter your account holder name.
  6. Enter your payment account number or details.
  7. Review your information and submit the request.
What is the minimum withdrawal amount?
The current withdrawal form has a minimum withdrawal amount of $5 USD. Your available balance must also be sufficient for the requested amount.
Which withdrawal methods are supported?
The current website code accepts:
  • JazzCash
  • Easypaisa
  • Bank Transfer
  • UPaisa
  • NayaPay
  • SadaPay
Only select a method that is available on your live Withdraw page.
Why is my withdrawal request not accepted?
Check the following:
  • Your requested amount meets the minimum.
  • You have enough available balance.
  • Your withdrawal method is valid.
  • Your account holder name is entered.
  • Your account number or payment details are correct.
Why is my withdrawal still pending?
Withdrawal requests require processing by the administrator. Check your withdrawal history for the latest status.
What happens if I enter incorrect payment details?
Incorrect account details can cause payment problems. Always double-check your account holder name and payment number before submitting a withdrawal request.

👛 Wallet & Transaction History

Where can I check my wallet balance?
Log in and open your dashboard. Your wallet information and available balance are shown in your account.
How do I view my transactions?
Open Transaction History or the corresponding wallet section. Your transactions are displayed with their type, amount and date, with the newest transactions shown first.
Why has my balance not updated?
Some account changes depend on request processing or administrator approval. Refresh your dashboard and check your transaction history. If the issue remains, contact support.
What if I see an unfamiliar transaction?
Review the transaction type, amount and date. If you do not recognize it, avoid sharing your account credentials and contact support for assistance.

🤝 Referral Program

What is the EarnHub Referral Program?
The referral program lets you invite other people to EarnHub using your personal referral link. Referral information and recorded referral earnings are available in your account.
Where can I find my referral link?
Log in and open the Referral section. Your personal referral link is generated using your account's referral code. Copy and share that link.
How can I check my referral earnings?
Open your Referral section to view your recorded referral count and referral earnings.
How much can I earn from referrals?
Referral amounts are determined by the active EarnHub referral settings. Check the current amount displayed in your account rather than relying on an old promotion or message.

🔔 Notifications

Where can I see my notifications?
Log in and open the Notifications section from your account dashboard.
How do I mark a notification as read?
Open your Notifications section and use the available mark-as-read option for the notification.
Why am I not seeing a notification?
Refresh your account and check the Notifications section again. Make sure you are logged in to the correct account.

🔐 Safety & Security

How can I keep my account safe?
  • Use a strong, unique password.
  • Never share your password with anyone.
  • Only use the official EarnHub website.
  • Check payment details before submitting requests.
  • Log out when using a shared computer.
Should I share my password or login details with support?
No. Never share your password or private login credentials with anyone.
Can I submit fake task proof?
No. Always submit genuine proof that follows the task instructions. Incorrect or misleading proof may result in rejection.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

The website is not loading. What should I do?
Check your internet connection, refresh the page and try opening EarnHub again. You can also try another browser.
My task link is not opening.
Check your browser's pop-up settings. The task opens in a new tab, so make sure pop-ups are not blocked.
My deposit screenshot is not uploading.
Make sure your image is JPG, PNG or WEBP and smaller than 5 MB. Check your internet connection and try again.
I cannot log in to my account.
Check that your email address and password are correct. Make sure there are no extra spaces in the email field.
What should I do if a feature is not working?
Refresh the page, check your internet connection and try again. If the problem continues, contact EarnHub support with a clear description of the issue. Do not share your password.
